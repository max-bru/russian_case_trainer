How to play

  1. You see a word or phrase in its basic form, plus a target case and singular/plural.
  2. The small cue word (e.g. с ___, у ___) shows the sentence that needs this case.
  3. Pick the correctly declined form with 1–4 or a click. Fast answers score more; streaks multiply your points.
  4. After a mistake, the correct ending is highlighted. Press Space to continue. Missed items come back a few questions later.
  5. Esc finishes the round. Menu goes back to settings.
